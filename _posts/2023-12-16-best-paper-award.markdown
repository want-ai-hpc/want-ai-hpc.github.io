We would like to thank all contributed authors for submitting their [papers](https://openreview.net/group?id=NeurIPS.cc/2023/Workshop/WANT)! The were many great papers and the selection process was not easy for the program committee 😅 

We are happy to announce that WANT@NeurIPS'23 best paper awards are going to the following papers:

- [Sparse Backpropagation for MoE Training (by Liyuan Liu, Jianfeng Gao, Weizhu Chen)](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=d1KZv5aVqn) 
- [Efficient Parallelization Layouts for Large-Scale Distributed Model Training (by Johannes Hagemann, Samuel Weinbach, Konstantin Dobler, Maximilian Schall, Gerard de Melo)](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=Y0AHNkVDeu)

Congratulations to the best paper award winners!
